Industrial Supply Infrastructure

B2B Commercial Packaging Tiers & Global Logistics Blueprint

Engineered for global distributors, food manufacturers, cosmetic brands, and private label partners. Flexible pack configurations and full export compliance.

Tier 01 • Retail Ready

Private Label Jars

Turnkey consumer-ready glass jars and cosmetic bottles with custom branded labels, tamper shrink sleeves, and barcode printing.

  • • Pack Sizes: 250ml, 500ml, 1000ml
  • • Master Carton: 12 or 24 units/box
  • • MOQ: 1,000 retail units
Tier 02 • Foodservice

Commercial Pails

Heavy-duty food-grade HDPE pails with airtight tear-strip lids and pouring spouts designed for artisan bakeries, restaurants, and soap makers.

  • • Pack Sizes: 5 Liters & 20 Liters
  • • Pallet Stack: 36 or 48 pails/pallet
  • • MOQ: 20 pails (400 Liters)
Tier 03 • Manufacturing

Standard Steel / HDPE Drums

UN-certified, food-grade epoxy-lined steel or high-density polyethylene drums for industrial food processing, cosmetics, and confectionery.

  • • Pack Sizes: 190kg net / 210L gross
  • • Pallet Stack: 4 drums/pallet
  • • MOQ: 4 drums (1 pallet)
Tier 04 • High Volume

Intermediate Bulk Containers (IBC)

Heavy-duty galvanized steel cage composite IBC totes equipped with bottom discharge valves and optional thermostatically-controlled heating jackets.

  • • Pack Sizes: 950kg net / 1000 Liters
  • • Container Load: 18–20 totes/20ft FCL
  • • MOQ: 1 IBC Tote
