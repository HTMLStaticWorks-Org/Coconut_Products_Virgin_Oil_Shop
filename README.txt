COCONUT PRODUCTS & VIRGIN OIL SHOP — WEBSITE PACKAGE

HOW TO PREVIEW
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. Use the navigation to visit Products, Our Story, Quality & Lab, Bulk Enquiry and Contact.
4. home-premium.html is the alternate Premium Edition homepage.

INCLUDED
- Responsive static multi-page website.
- User-provided coconut illustration used as the brand mark.
- Generated local product photography.
- Product category filters.
- Dark/light mode toggle.
- Mobile navigation menu.
- Product quick-enquiry links.
- Wholesale and contact enquiry forms (mailto demo).
- Matching brand palette: palm green #1B4332 and coconut ochre #C59B27.

IMPORTANT BEFORE GOING LIVE
- Replace the demo brand name "COCONUT & CO." with the actual business name.
- Replace hello@example.com, phone number and address with real contact details.
- Confirm which products are actually sold and remove suggested catalogue items that are unavailable.
- Add real product prices, pack sizes, ingredients, nutrition/specification details and shipping policies.
- Add only valid licences, certifications and actual laboratory reports. No certification or test claims are asserted by this demo.
- The forms currently open the visitor's email application. For automatic enquiry collection, connect a backend or form service.
- This is a front-end static website. It does not include a secure payment gateway, customer accounts, inventory database or order-management backend.
- Google Fonts are loaded from the internet; the local photographs and brand mark are included in the ZIP.
