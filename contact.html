Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing, product specifications, minimum quantities, export procedures, and private label partnership with Coconut & Co.

For trial orders and testing batches, our minimum order quantity starts from 1 carton (e.g., 24 glass jars or 10 kg). For commercial wholesale supply, orders typically begin at 1 pallet (approx. 800 kg or 4 standard 200L drums). We also accommodate 20ft (14–16 MT) and 40ft (22–24 MT) full container loads (FCL) with mixed product allocations.

Yes, we export worldwide via Cochin Sea Port (140km away) and air cargo hubs. Every commercial export consignment is accompanied by a full dossier including Certificate of Analysis (COA), Phytosanitary Certificate, Certificate of Origin, Non-GMO Declaration, Bill of Lading, and custom commercial invoices adhering to international customs guidelines.

Qualified businesses, commercial kitchens, cosmetic formulators, and research laboratories can request evaluation samples. Sample kits (100ml – 500ml glass jars for oils, and 250g vacuum packs for desiccated coconut and flour) are dispatched via express courier within 48 hours of verification.

Yes. We offer contract packaging and private labeling services for brand owners and retailers. Options include custom glass jars (200ml to 1L), aluminum bottles, food-grade HDPE containers, standing barrier pouches, and bulk IBC totes. We assist with label compliance, barcode application, and tamper-evident shrink sealing.

Our virgin coconut oil is purely cold-pressed from fresh, mature coconut meat within 48 hours of harvest. We never use chemical solvents (0% hexane), artificial deodorizers, or bleaching clays. The resulting oil maintains a crystal-clear diamond transparency when liquid, rich natural lauric acid content (50%+), and fresh sweet coconut aroma.

Yes, prospective bulk buyers, private label clients, and third-party quality inspectors are welcome to visit our Pollachi Agro-Corridor processing center. Please contact our coordination desk at least 48 hours in advance so our technical director can arrange a complete facility tour, plantation walkthrough, and sample testing session.
